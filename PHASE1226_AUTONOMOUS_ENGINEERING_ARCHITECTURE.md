# Phase 1226: Autonomous Software Engineering & End-State Architecture

## 1. Operating Paradigm Shift
Phase 1226 establishes WebOS as a **Goal-Driven Autonomous Software Engineering System** operating under the **Goal Owner** paradigm:
- **Human Role (Goal Owner):** Expresses destinations, objectives, priorities, constraints, and preferences. Does NOT prescribe filenames, classes, functions, or implementation steps.
- **WebOS Role (Cognitive Architect & Autonomous System):**
  1. `UNDERSTANDING`: Parses natural language intent into concrete requirements and constraints.
  2. `ARCHITECTING`: Models desired vs. current system architecture, capability graphs, and dependency trees.
  3. `REASONING & PLANNING`: Ingests software architecture knowledge, evaluates candidate options, and derives prioritized roadmaps.
  4. `GOVERNED MUTATION`: Submits execution plans through PrimeGate for policy governance.
  5. `BUILD & VALIDATION`: Executes real compilation and physical GUI/process validation.
  6. `OBSERVABILITY`: Exposes human-readable progress telemetry (`✓ Understanding`, `● Comparing options`, `○ Validating`) and realistic ETAs over HTTP port 8725.
  7. `LEARNING & EVOLUTION`: Consolidates outcome evidence into experience memory to continuously improve future decisions.
