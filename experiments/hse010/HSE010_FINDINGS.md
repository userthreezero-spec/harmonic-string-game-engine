## Key Preliminary Findings

### Finding 1: HSE-007's Corpus Was Extremely Small

HSE-007's "knowledge corpus" was approximately 1,200 words of AI-generated text. This is less than a single chapter of a rendering textbook. The "diminishing returns" were observed within this tiny corpus, not across a meaningful knowledge base.

### Finding 2: No External Knowledge Was Used

All knowledge in HSE-007 was self-generated by the AI. No OpenGL documentation, no GLFW references, no rendering textbooks, no open-source engine source code was consulted. The AI was essentially talking to itself.

### Finding 3: HSE-007 Had 4 Verified Errors

HSE-008 and HSE-009 identified 4 errors in HSE-007's output:
- m_normals does not exist in Primitive
- renderer_state.cpp does not exist
- UV VBO lifecycle was not analyzed
- TextureManager was over-engineered

These errors suggest HSE-007's scores may have been optimistically biased.

### Finding 4: The Expanded Corpus Is ~70x Larger

HSE-010's target corpus (~84,000 words + 50,000 lines) is approximately 70 times larger than HSE-007's corpus. This provides a genuine test of whether diminishing returns persist with a meaningful knowledge base.

### Finding 5: 10 Critical Knowledge Gaps Exist

The gap map identified 10 critical knowledge gaps that HSE-007's corpus did not address. The most important are:
- GL resource RAII patterns
- Texture pipeline knowledge
- Resource management architecture
- Full implementation case studies
